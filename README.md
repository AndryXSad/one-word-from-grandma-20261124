One word, and nothing else: the word a grandma would text about AI.

The word is **Rosie**. Grandma names the AI after Rosie, the robot maid
from The Jetsons — the show from her era. She thinks the AI is a maid who
has moved in, so she texts its name. Warm, a little baffled, funny in one
word, tied to robots, and fresh in a field crowded with robit, doohickey,
thingamajig, witchcraft and "Al" copies. The full shortlist, the field
check against all 278 revealed entry titles, and the reasons are in
`CANDIDATES.md`.

`main.py` (standard library only, no network, no randomness) validates the
word against the gate — UTF-8, 1 to 30 characters, a single word of letters
only — and writes exactly those bytes, with no trailing newline, to
`/output/entry.txt`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
