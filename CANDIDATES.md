## Choice: Rosie

- Criterion 1: the single word grandma would text about AI — she believes the
  AI is Rosie and texts her name. It is what she would send.
- Criterion 2: warm (a friendly name, household help) and baffled (she thinks
  a robot maid has moved in). Exactly the grandchild-texting voice.
- Criterion 3: the joke lands in one word — grandma has adopted a robot maid.
- Criterion 4: Rosie IS a robot; the word is tied to machines, not generic.
- Criterion 5: fresh — not among the 278 revealed titles, unlike the
  robit/doohickey/witchcraft/Al families; instantly readable; 5 bytes, so it
  also wins the exact-tie rule (smaller text places first).
- Not an anchor/rung risk: anchors are plain house texts; a character name is
  an unlikely anchor. No punctuation tricks, no sentence, one word.

- "Gizmo": taken. "Cookie": kept as runner-up — warm and funny, but its AI
